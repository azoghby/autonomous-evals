# Sandbox quality outcomes and monetary telemetry

`docs/SANDBOX_EVALS_PLAN.md` §10 makes cost a reward gate only when the customer sets a budget. The factory records `outcomeRule: sandbox-quality-outcome-v2` and binds the trusted approved quality cost policy into private reports and durable integrated-trial replay identity.

A completed execution with known infrastructure and complete passing SQL, trajectory, security, output, and applicable media evidence can produce a statistical pass while monetary telemetry remains `{status: 'unknown', microUsd: null}`. Unknown money is never projected as zero or a numeric legacy trial cost. Spending authorization and external reservation/hold requirements continue independently of quality; a customer quality budget does not replace operational spending admission.

The server-held `CustomerSuiteApproval` may include `qualityCostPolicy: {version: 'sandbox-quality-cost-policy-v1', maxCostMicroUsd: <nonnegative safe integer>}`. Only the trusted approval resolver supplies this policy. A run request cannot remove it. Approval storage must be immutable and reject conflicting replacements; the integrated replay fence also rejects a changed approval or policy under the same trial identity before execution. With this policy, unavailable settled cost leaves the outcome unknown. Invalid monetary values and incomplete execution or grading cannot pass.

Failures retain unknown failure attribution until an authoritative attribution path establishes a cause. A failed grade therefore remains excluded from the agent failure denominator. The implementation does not infer agent responsibility from a failed assertion.

Historical private reports retain their stored outcomes. New reports use the versioned rule and policy digest; replay conflicts rather than rewriting an old outcome or dispatching the trial again. Statistical summaries reject mixtures of rule or policy identities. New reports require a new trial identity when intentionally changing policy.

Generation gate execution similarly accepts unknown cost without an explicit quality cost policy, rejects invalid costs and incomplete executions, and binds the versioned rule and policy into newly emitted gate evidence. Legacy evidence is validated using its original cost requirement. Gate admission remains a separate requirement from customer suite approval.
