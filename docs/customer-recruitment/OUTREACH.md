## Offer and implementation truth

Start with Trace Audit: a customer-authorized, redacted transcript or tool log, at most 200 KB, checked for unsupported success claims, ignored tool errors and instruction violations. Findings must distinguish confirmed evidence from concerns and inconclusive results. A trace audit cannot establish what happened outside the supplied log or prove overall agent reliability.

PLAN.md lists $5 pilot Trace Audit, $5 Text/Workflow Eval, $10 comparison and $49/month monitoring. Its schedule also mentions free first audits; no free offer is authorized here. Use the listed $5 price only as a proposed pilot price, and take no payment until the plan's acceptance gates pass. Do not promise turnaround or a finding.

README.md and docs/track-b-release-status.md describe implemented core services: Naïve sessions, Trace Audit, secure targets, Text/Calendar execution, evidence/reporting, remediation and comparison/monitoring components. Source includes draft-only commercial outreach. Retained synthetic Trace Audit evidence caught one false-success fixture and produced no finding on a successful-tool control. These are narrow historical checks, not customer acceptance.

Application composition, real authentication/private storage, deployment/configuration, rich end-to-end acceptance and live payment completion are not established by the reviewed evidence. Monitoring alerts are drafts in the documented core; notification delivery is not implemented there. Do not market a fully deployed autonomous service, production-ready endpoint testing, working subscriptions, proven savings or existing customers. This review read source/status records; it did not rerun tests or verify a deployment.
